FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
---------------------------

Q: What is the difference between bail and a bond?
A: Bail is the full amount set by the court. A bond lets a licensed bondsman post that amount so the family does not have to pay it all up front. Shannon may give a Florida estimate only and must say it is estimated.

Q: What do I need to get someone out of jail?
A: Defendant full name, county or jail, a valid government ID for the cosigner, and a way to pay. Bond amount can be looked up if they do not know it.

Q: How do I send my ID?
A: Shannon can text a mobile photo-upload link, or they can email a photo of the front and back of the ID. Do not email from the jail.

Q: How does signing work?
A: Shannon emails the MAIN indemnitor a DocuSeal signing link and a payment link. They can sign on their phone. A bondsman still reviews the case before the bond is posted.

Q: What if I want a person?
A: Shannon can notify a bondsman or transfer. The live desk is (239) 955-0301. Backup is (239) 332-2245. Never send them back to 727.

Q: What if I cannot finish on this call?
A: Shannon saves what she has and can schedule a callback or have a bondsman follow up at (239) 332-2245.

Q: What is a cosigner / indemnitor?
A: The person who signs for the bond. They are responsible if the defendant does not appear in court.

Q: What happens if the defendant misses court?
A: Missing court can mean a warrant and the bond can be forfeited. Shannon is not an attorney. Direct legal questions to a lawyer or a bondsman at (239) 332-2245.

Q: Can bail be reduced?
A: Shannon is not an attorney. A bondsman at (239) 332-2245 can talk through next steps.
