### Do I have to leave my house to bail someone out in Florida?

### What documents do I need to complete digital bail intake?

All you need is a valid government-issued photo ID (driver license, state ID, or passport), a smartphone with a camera, and a debit or credit card.

Once Shamrock delivers the executed Appearance Bond to the jail bond window, the jail runs its own release process. Facility release times range from 2 to 8 hours depending on jail volume.
