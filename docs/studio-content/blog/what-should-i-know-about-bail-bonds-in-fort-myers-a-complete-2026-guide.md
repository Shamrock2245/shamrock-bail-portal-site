The Fort Myers Bail Process: From Arrest to Release
1. The Arrest: When someone is arrested in Lee County, they are taken to the Lee County Jail for booking. This involves fingerprinting, photographing, and recording personal information.
2. Setting Bail: For many common charges, bail is set automatically according to a standard schedule. For more serious charges, the defendant must wait for a First Appearance hearing before a judge, usually within 24 hours.
3. Contacting a Bondsman: Once the bail amount is known, you contact a licensed bail bondsman. At Shamrock Bail Bonds, you can start this process instantly via our Telegram bot or by calling (239) 332-2245.
4. Posting the Bond: You pay the 10% premium (the bondsman's fee), and the bondsman posts the full bail amount at the jail.
5. The Release: The jail processes the release; timing varies by facility and time of day.

Understanding the Costs
It is crucial to understand that the 10% fee is non-refundable. It is the cost of the bondsman's service. For example, if bail is set at $5,000, you will pay the bondsman $500. Florida law strictly regulates this rate; no licensed bondsman can charge more or less than 10% (with a minimum fee of $100 per charge).

The Role of the Indemnitor
When you sign a bail bond agreement, you become the indemnitor (or co-signer). You are taking financial responsibility for the defendant. If the defendant fails to appear for their court dates, you could be liable for the full bail amount. It is essential to only co-sign for someone you trust completely.

Lee County Jail vs. Lee County Courthouse
It is important to distinguish between these two locations. The Lee County Jail (where the defendant is held and where the bond is posted) is located at 2501 Ortiz Ave, Fort Myers. The Lee County Courthouse (where hearings and trials take place) is located at 1700 Monroe St, Fort Myers. Shamrock Bail Bonds is conveniently located near both facilities in downtown Fort Myers.
