## Options for Posting Bail in Sarasota County

Once a judge has set a bail amount, you have a few options to secure a release:

- Cash Bail: You can pay the full bail amount directly to the Sarasota County Sheriff's Office. This can be done online through their official payment portal or by phone [2]. The full amount is returned at the conclusion of the case, provided the defendant has made all required court appearances.
- Surety Bond (Bail Bond): This is the most common and financially manageable option. You partner with a licensed bail bond company, such as Shamrock Bail Bonds. You pay the company a non-refundable premium, which is legally set at 10% of the total bail amount in Florida. The bail bond company then posts the full bond with the court, guaranteeing the defendant's appearance.
